DAY TRACKER — ENGLISH TEXT, FOR FINAL APPROVAL

Everything a patient or a member of staff reads, grouped by where it appears.
Settle this first; the eight translations are then made from it once, rather
than chasing it.

Mark anything you want changed. Lines marked SHORT appear on a button and
cannot grow much without breaking the layout.

==================================================================
1 · CHOOSING A LANGUAGE  (first screen on a new phone)
==================================================================

1. languageTitle
   Which language?

2. languageHint
   This can be changed later from the menu.

==================================================================
2 · RED  (shown during onboarding, and from the menu)
==================================================================

3. offName
   Red — you are off

4. offDesc [SHORT]
   Slow, tremor, stiff or frozen

5. offFull
   Stiff or frozen. Hard to get up from a chair or from bed. Movements feel slow. Or a shaking tremor.

6. offExample
   Your feet feel stuck to the floor, or you need help to stand up. That is red.

==================================================================
3 · GREEN
==================================================================

7. onName
   Green — you are on

8. onDesc [SHORT]
   Moving and working easily

9. onFull
   You get up, walk and do all your work fairly easily. You do not need anyone's help.

10. onExample
   This is your good period. Most of the day should be green.

==================================================================
4 · BLUE  (only when dyskinesia tracking is on)
==================================================================

11. extraName
   Blue — too much

12. extraDesc [SHORT]
   Unwanted extra movements

13. extraFull
   Extra movements you cannot control, getting in the way of what you are doing.

14. extraExample
   Your body, head or arms move on their own, so eating or sitting still is difficult.

==================================================================
5 · HOW THE ALARMS WORK  (onboarding)
==================================================================

15. alarmsTitle
   The alarm rings

16. alarmsLine1
   Once every hour while you are awake, to ask you for a colour.

17. alarmsLine2
   And again at each tablet time, to remind you to take that tablet.

18. alarmsLine3
   You can also press a colour any time, without waiting for the alarm.

15 · THE STAFF SETUP WIZARD  (English only, filled by doctor or nurse)
==================================================================

80. staffTitle
   Set up for the patient

81. staffIntro
   Fill this in with the patient before handing over the phone. One tablet at a time.

82. staffBegin
   Start

83. askName
   What is the tablet called?

84. askNameHint
   Write the name once. It is not asked again.

85. askDose
   How much is taken each time?

86. askDoseHint
   For example: 1 tablet, half tablet.

87. askFreq
   How many times a day?

88. askFreqHint
   The number of doses in a day.

89. askTimes
   At what times?

90. askTimesHint
   Use the patient's usual timings. These repeat every day.

91. askMore
   Any other tablet?

92. addAnotherTablet
   Yes, add another tablet

93. noMoreTablets
   No, that is all

94. askDyskinesia

95. dyskinesiaExplain
   By default the patient answers with two colours only — red when off, green when on. Turn this on to add a third colour for dyskinesia, when that is the question you are trying to answer.

96. dyskinesiaYes
   Yes, add the blue button

97. dyskinesiaNo
   No, two colours only

98. wakingTitle
   When are you awake?

99. wakingStaffHint
   Ask the patient. The app only asks for a colour between these hours, and these also set the suggested tablet times.

100. setupTitle
   Your tablets

101. setupFootnote
   Once saved the timings are locked in and repeat every day.

102. handOver
   Save and hand over

103. backStep
