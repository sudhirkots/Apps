DAY TRACKER — TRANSLATION CHECK
ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada)

The app asks a Parkinson's patient one question every hour: how are
you feeling right now? They answer by tapping a colour. These are the
words that explain the colours.

Please read each one against the English above it. Reply with the
NUMBER and your correction. Anything you do not mention stays as it is.

What matters is not grammar but sense: whether a patient would
understand it the same way a doctor means it.

RED — the medicine is not working
==========================================================

1. HEADING
   (the title of this colour, shown above the explanation)
   English:  Red — Off period
   Current:  ಕೆಂಪು — ಔಷಧದ ಪರಿಣಾಮ ಇಲ್ಲ

2. BUTTON
   (the short line on the button the patient taps every hour — must stay short)
   English:  Slow, tremor, stiff or frozen
   Current:  ನಿಧಾನ, ನಡುಕ, ಬಿಗಿತ ಅಥವಾ ಸ್ತಬ್ಧತೆ

3. DEFINITION
   (the main explanation of what this colour means)
   English:  Stiff or frozen. Hard to get up from a chair or from bed. Movements feel slow. Or a shaking tremor.
   Current:  ಬಿಗಿತ ಅಥವಾ ಸ್ತಬ್ಧವಾದಂತೆ. ಕುರ್ಚಿಯಿಂದ ಅಥವಾ ಹಾಸಿಗೆಯಿಂದ ಏಳುವುದು ಕಷ್ಟ. ಚಲನೆ ನಿಧಾನ. ಅಥವಾ ನಡುಕ.

4. EXAMPLE
   (a concrete illustration, shown in italics under the definition)
   English:  Your feet feel stuck to the floor, or you need help to stand up. That is red.
   Current:  ಕಾಲುಗಳು ನೆಲಕ್ಕೆ ಅಂಟಿಕೊಂಡಂತೆ ಅನಿಸುತ್ತದೆ, ಅಥವಾ ಏಳಲು ಸಹಾಯ ಬೇಕಾಗುತ್ತದೆ. ಅದೇ ಕೆಂಪು.

GREEN — the medicine is working
==========================================================

5. HEADING
   (the title of this colour, shown above the explanation)
   English:  Green — On period
   Current:  ಹಸಿರು — ಔಷಧ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಿದೆ

6. BUTTON
   (the short line on the button the patient taps every hour — must stay short)

   Current:  ಸುಲಭವಾಗಿ ಓಡಾಟ ಮತ್ತು ಕೆಲಸ

8. EXAMPLE
   (a concrete illustration, shown in italics under the definition)
   English:  This is your good period. Most of the day should be green.
   Current:  ಇದು ನಿಮ್ಮ ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಸಮಯ. ದಿನದ ಹೆಚ್ಚಿನ ಭಾಗ ಹಸಿರಾಗಿರಬೇಕು.

BLUE — too much movement
==========================================================

9. HEADING
   (the title of this colour, shown above the explanation)
   English:  Blue — Dyskinesia
   Current:  ನೀಲಿ — ತುಂಬಾ ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಚಲನೆ

10. BUTTON
   (the short line on the button the patient taps every hour — must stay short)
   English:  Unwanted extra movements
   Current:  ಬೇಡವಾದ ಹೆಚ್ಚುವರಿ ಚಲನೆ

11. DEFINITION
   (the main explanation of what this colour means)
   English:  Unwanted and extra movements that you cannot control. They get in the way of what you are doing.
   Current:  ನಿಮ್ಮ ಹಿಡಿತದಲ್ಲಿಲ್ಲದ ಹೆಚ್ಚುವರಿ ಚಲನೆಗಳು, ಅವು ನಿಮ್ಮ ಕೆಲಸಕ್ಕೆ ಅಡ್ಡಿಯಾಗುತ್ತವೆ.

12. EXAMPLE
   (a concrete illustration, shown in italics under the definition)
   English:  Your body, head or arms move on their own, so eating or sitting still is difficult.
   Current:  ದೇಹ, ತಲೆ ಅಥವಾ ಕೈಗಳು ತಾವಾಗಿಯೇ ಅಲುಗಾಡುತ್ತವೆ, ಇದರಿಂದ ಊಟ ಮಾಡುವುದು ಅಥವಾ ಸುಮ್ಮನೆ ಕೂರುವುದು ಕಷ್ಟ.
